**To:** Farm Management
**From:** Farm Operations Analyst
**Date:** November 7, 2026
**Subject:** Recommended Planting Allocation

## Recommendation

I recommend planting 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds. This allocation uses 60 of the 64 available beds and generates approximately $42,761.66 in seasonal profit.

## Why This Allocation

Tomatoes generate the highest revenue per bed, but their labor requirements increase quickly. The marginal cost of the eleventh tomato bed exceeds its additional revenue, making further tomato production less attractive.

The recommended allocation also stays within the farm's available labor capacity. Using all available land is not necessary when additional production would reduce profit.
