# AI Conventions

## How I Use AI

I use AI to help me understand concepts, organize ideas, check my work, improve formatting, and troubleshoot problems.

AI may help me draft mechanical or setup files for this repository. For graded coursework, I will complete my own first draft before using AI to provide feedback or help improve my work.

## My Working Preferences

- Explain unfamiliar concepts step by step and in simple language.
- Show calculations and explain how answers were reached.
- Point out mistakes instead of automatically agreeing with me.
- Keep responses clear and concise unless more detail is needed.
- Ask me before making assumptions about missing information.

## Before I Commit

Before committing AI-assisted work to GitHub, I will:

- Read and review the content myself.
- Verify calculations and factual information.
- Make sure the work accurately represents my own knowledge and decisions.
- Check that no sensitive or confidential information is included.

## Never Include

I will not paste into AI or commit to this public repository:

- Confidential employer information
- Personally identifiable information belonging to other people
- Passwords, login credentials, API keys, or access tokens
- Course materials that I do not have permission to publicly share

If information may be sensitive or inappropriate for a public repository, I will verify that it is safe to use before including it.

## Prompt Log

When I use AI to help change files in this repository, I will document the interaction in `prompt-log.md`.
