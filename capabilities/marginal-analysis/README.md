# Marginal Analysis

## Purpose

This capability demonstrates how marginal analysis can be used to make business decisions by comparing additional revenue with additional costs while considering resource constraints and diminishing returns.

## Key Findings

The model recommends planting 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds, generating approximately $42,761.66 in seasonal profit.

The analysis demonstrates that maximizing profit does not necessarily require using every available resource. It also shows how diminishing returns, marginal costs, and labor expenses influence production decisions.

## What I Learned

I learned that generating more revenue does not always result in higher profit. Even though tomatoes produce the highest revenue per bed, their increasing labor requirements make additional production less profitable. I also learned how binding and nonbinding constraints affect decisions about allocating limited resources.
